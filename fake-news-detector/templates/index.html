How It Works

Our AI analyzes your text in three steps

01
📝

Text Preprocessing

Your article text is cleaned and normalized, removing noise while preserving meaningful linguistic patterns.

02
🧮

TF-IDF Vectorization

The text is converted into numerical feature vectors using Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency, capturing word importance.

03
🤖

ML Classification

A Passive Aggressive Classifier trained on thousands of articles predicts the verdict with a confidence score.

About This Project

This tool uses a machine learning pipeline built with Python's scikit-learn library. The model is trained on labeled news datasets using a combination of TF-IDF vectorization and a Passive Aggressive Classifier.

While AI can help identify patterns common in fake news, it is not perfect. Always verify important news through multiple trusted sources.
